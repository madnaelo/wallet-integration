# Buyer Search Intent

Research date: 2026-09-24. Live Google search for `white label crypto swap software` and Bing search for `wallet swap integration`, plus primary competitor pages. Rankings vary by location/session. No search-volume, conversion-rate or ranking forecasts were purchased or inferred.

## Findings

- Google results mixed complete exchanges (HollaEx, AlphaPoint, Shift Markets), wallet software and ChangeNOW. Do not compete by claiming custody, liquidity, banking or a complete exchange.
- Bing wallet-integration results included ChangeNOW's integration guide, the Uniswap/Ledger integration case and SwapKit. Technical buyers expect API/SDK documentation. Our offer is an existing application plus scoped integration, not a published drop-in SDK.
- Agencies are the initial outreach segment: repeated wallet/DeFi project work creates a plausible reuse case. This is a hypothesis to validate in conversations, not established demand.

## Positioning Rules

Sell reusable software, branded deployment and scoped engineering. Do not call the offer a centralized exchange, broker, custodian, liquidity provider, market-maker, investment adviser, trading bot or ready-to-install SDK. No invented delivery savings, customers, reviews, security certifications, pricing, profit or legal clearance. Radar public demonstration remains synthetic; private Binance research is not a customer data license.

## Research Qualification

`prospects.csv` contains 50 public-source sector-fit candidates, not 50 sales-qualified opportunities. Fit is our inference from their advertised work. Country values explicitly distinguish offices from verified incorporation; `Not verified` is intentional. Public business channels are not blanket consent. No private email database was used. The first batch is restricted to individually checked UK corporate business inboxes; no retail financial promotion or UAE/Pakistan-targeted campaign.

Check current company status, applicable marketing rules, objections and contact channel before each send. No automated follow-up, purchased list, tracking pixel or mass blast. A positive reply with a relevant project is required to count a qualified demo request.
