## Content Decision

Most white-label results sell full exchanges, whereas integration results emphasize providers/APIs. Clarify that our offer is a reusable **application** with branded deployment, not a matching engine, liquidity source or public SDK. Improve two existing guides instead of generating near-duplicate keyword pages:

- Build/license: delivery comparison across widget, API build and licensed application; ownership, retained work and an illustrative scope comparison, with no invented price or time-saving claim.
- Wallet integration: concrete account-switch, rejection, expiry, allowance, cross-chain pending and duplicate-report acceptance cases.
- Bring the software deliverable forward on the buyer pages. Keep operator, provider and license boundaries in supporting sections.

Primary technical sources inspected: [0x Swap API](https://0x.org/products/swap) and [Uniswap integration guide](https://developers.uniswap.org/docs/trading/swapping-api/start-building/integration-guide). Their interfaces are not our application license, and referencing them does not enable a provider.

## Recheck Method

Repeat the same queries, location context and first-page observation weekly. Record query-level changes without attributing causation to one edit. Add Search Console impressions/clicks when reporting becomes available. Do not use page-one absence as an indexing diagnostic or promise a ranking deadline.
