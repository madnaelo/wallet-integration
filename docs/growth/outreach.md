## Qualify, Do Not Overclaim

- One concrete response expressing interest in a relevant demo is the first milestone, not an open, click, auto-reply or test form submission.
- Ask about the product/client need, the contact's role, intended networks, deployment ownership and timing. Discuss paid-pilot scope only after relevance is established.
- No fixed delivery savings, security certification, trading return or commercial live-Radar licence is implied.
- Use campaign-level tags only. Do not add email addresses or prospect-specific identifiers to links. No tracking pixels, purchased lists or automated follow-up sequence.
- Actual messages, mail IDs, delivery failures, replies and objections belong in the ignored private `.dev/growth/` records, not the public repository. A successful send is not proof of delivery or interest.
