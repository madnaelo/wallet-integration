## Scope And Deliverables

One customer brand [ ], one customer-owned deployment [accounts/project IDs],
existing modules [explicit list], networks/providers [explicit bounded list].
Deliver branding/configuration, isolated frontend/backend/database deployment,
acceptance report, build/run instructions, configuration inventory without secrets,
handover and the defect window defined in [the current offer](pilot-offer.md).

## Responsibilities

Seller configures existing software, reviews configuration boundaries, tests the
agreed workflow, documents limitations and hands over access. Seller does not
hold user funds, operate the customer's business or provide legal approvals.

Buyer supplies branding rights, infrastructure, domains, provider approvals and
accounts, secret delivery through an agreed secure channel, fee recipient ownership,
an acceptance owner and applicable permissions/privacy notices. Never send private
keys or seed phrases. Buyer funds and signs any expressly authorized real-money
acceptance tests; the public demo is not a settlement/payout test.

## Acceptance

1. Branding and named modules match the agreed scope on desktop/mobile.
2. Demo/preview cannot submit real orders or swaps; live activation is a separate
   written decision after prerequisites, not an effect of deployment.
3. Network/provider/recipient configuration passes preflight; isolated resource
   identities do not reuse seller/other-customer secrets, databases or treasury.
4. Automated tests and agreed acceptance cases [attach list] pass. Limitations,
   evidence gaps and external dependencies are recorded, not hidden.
5. Access, build/run documentation, backup/restore and handover are delivered.

Buyer reviews within [5 business days proposed] and reports reproducible deviations.
Acceptance is recorded in writing; silence is not automatically acceptance.
Timeline [ ] starts only after prerequisites; third-party approval delays are outside
delivery control. Changes require written scope/price agreement.

## Commercial And Support Boundary

Use [pilot-offer.md](pilot-offer.md) as the price/milestone/support source when
preparing a dated quote. Freeze agreed price [ ], taxes [ ], deposit [ ], final [ ],
invoice/payment method [ ] in the signed agreement. No payment collection built in.
Non-exclusive core license, permitted deployments, source/modification rights,
redistribution restrictions and ownership of bespoke work must be agreed separately.
Keep applicable third-party license notices; do not sublicense rights we lack.

Defect correction covers reproducible nonconformance with signed acceptance criteria
within the agreed window. Response schedule [ ] and contact [ ] must be agreed;
no implied 24/7 SLA, ongoing hosting, new features or indefinite updates.

Excluded unless separately agreed: new protocols/chains, bespoke contracts, custody,
liquidity, regulatory/legal work, approval fees, customer acquisition, multi-tenancy,
unlimited development and ongoing 24/7 operations. Software licensing is not legal
clearance. Required commercial/legal review remains a prerequisite to contracting.
