# Prospect Qualification

Contact a small number of researched teams, not a purchased contact list.

**Good fit:** existing Web3 product/community and audience; identifiable founder,
product owner or CTO; missing or demonstrably weak/manual swaps; a business reason
to retain users in-product; ability to obtain provider accounts/permissions; a
technical owner able to operate a deployment; budget for the current paid pilot.

**Reject:** individual traders, no product/users, anonymous speculative tokens,
requests for custody or market manipulation, evasive/deceptive conduct, attempts
to bypass provider/regulatory requirements, or no identified operator/budget.

Before quoting, ask: Which product/users? Who operates it and where? Which existing
networks/providers? Who owns infrastructure and obtains approvals? Who accepts the
delivery? Budget and target timing? Any custom requirement outside existing scope?
Do not ask for private keys, seed phrases, account passwords or sensitive user data.

Record verified evidence and dates, not assumptions presented as facts. Respect
opt-outs. Keep business contact data minimal and delete stale/unneeded leads.
Use the [tracker template](sales-tracker.md). Pricing/support lives in
[pilot-offer.md](pilot-offer.md), not in a new price list.

Next milestones: one qualified demo request; one paid-pilot discussion; first
payment under an agreed scope/license. Do not add features without buyer evidence.
