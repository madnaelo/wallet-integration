# Swap Assistant For Teams

**A branded non-custodial swap product, without rebuilding the complete workflow.**

For Web3 product teams, wallet/community products and agencies with an existing
audience and an identified operator. Wallet handoffs, token/network selection,
inconsistent quotes, transaction review, delivery and alerts take more than one API.

Swap Assistant brings together wallet connectivity, normalized route comparison,
supported same-chain/cross-chain swaps, transparent fee review, authenticated
activity, favorites and alerts. Private operations reporting distinguishes expected,
accrued and received fees using independent evidence, not browser success messages.
Availability depends on supported routes, approved accounts and configured channels.

**Users keep their private keys.** They review/sign swaps in their own wallets;
the application does not custody their principal or sign swaps for them. Supported
limit orders use advance user-signed, protocol-enforced terms, not blanket authority.
The public demo uses only synthetic prices and a sample wallet. It sends nothing.

**Delivery:** one brand and isolated customer-owned deployment, a named supported
network/provider scope, branding/configuration, acceptance tests, build/run docs,
deployment and handover. Your team supplies provider accounts, secrets, payout
configuration and required permissions. We retain the reusable core and license
it non-exclusively, with setup/integration scoped separately.
