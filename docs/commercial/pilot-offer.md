# Pilot Offer: Single Pricing Source

Internal commercial hypothesis, 2026-09-22. No market validation, buyer commitment,
revenue guarantee or regulatory clearance is implied. Change pricing here only;
other sales materials reference this file.

- Test pilot price: **AED 5,000**, subject to a written quote and applicable taxes.
- Offer: non-exclusive licensed deployment plus separately scoped setup/integration.
- Scope: one brand, one isolated customer-owned deployment; existing capabilities
  only; an explicitly listed, limited subset of already-supported networks/providers.
- Includes branding/configuration, acceptance testing, deployment, build/run docs
  and handover. Buyer supplies approved provider accounts, infrastructure and secrets.
- Defect window: 14 calendar days after written acceptance, covering reproducible
  deviations from the agreed acceptance criteria. Not upgrades or changed scope.
- Proposed milestones: 50% on signed scope/license; 50% on acceptance. These are
  negotiation assumptions, not an issued invoice or accepted contract.
- No subscription platform or managed hosting is included. Hosting/provider/domain
  charges are the buyer's costs; do not promise free infrastructure forever.
- Core IP retained by the seller. Source access, modification, deployment rights,
  dependency notices and redistribution limits must be specified in the license.

Excluded: new protocols/chains, custody, bespoke smart contracts, liquidity,
regulatory/legal services, customer acquisition, multi-tenancy, 24/7 operations,
unlimited development and third-party approval fees. Any extra work needs a quote.

Do not take payment or promise a go-live date before checking operator eligibility,
scope, dependency licensing and customer prerequisites. This is a commercial draft,
not legal advice or an exemption from applicable requirements.
