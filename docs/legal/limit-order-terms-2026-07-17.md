# Swap Assistant Limit Order Terms

Version: 2026-07-17.1

Effective date: July 17, 2026

This file is the archived wording associated with limit orders stored with
terms version 2026-07-17.1. It must not be edited after release. A wording
change requires a new version and a new archive file.

By accepting these terms and submitting a signed limit order, the user
acknowledges the following:

1. The user's wallet signs exact order terms, including the token pair, amount,
   minimum receive amount, recipient, network, and expiry. Swap Assistant
   cannot change a provider-verifiable signed order without invalidating it.
2. The signed order authorizes a supported third-party protocol or its solvers
   to fill the order within those signed terms before expiry. It does not give
   Swap Assistant custody of funds or access to the user's private key.
3. Execution is not guaranteed. A displayed or briefly observed market price
   does not guarantee sufficient liquidity or a fill.
4. Balance, token allowance, gas economics, liquidity, solver availability,
   smart contracts, networks, and third-party services can delay or prevent a
   fill.
5. A token approval is a separate onchain permission. It can remain active
   until used, replaced, or revoked, and approving or revoking it can require a
   network fee.
6. An eligible order can fill at any time before expiry, including while the
   user is away from Swap Assistant.
7. Cancellation is not complete until confirmed by the relevant protocol or
   blockchain. An order can fill while cancellation is pending.
8. Blockchain and smart-contract activity can be irreversible and involves the
   risk of partial or total loss. Swap Assistant does not provide financial,
   investment, tax, accounting, or legal advice.
9. The user is responsible for reviewing wallet prompts, maintaining balances
   and approvals, monitoring open orders, and cancelling orders or revoking
   approvals when appropriate.
10. Third-party provider terms, fees, availability, and protocol behavior also
    apply. Swap Assistant does not guarantee uninterrupted availability or any
    particular outcome.
